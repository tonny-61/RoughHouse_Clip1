# Clip 1 asset manifest

All visual and animation assets currently used by the Clip 1 prototype come from the Unreal Engine 5.8 installation on this workstation.

## Epic Games template assets

- Manny and Quinn simple skeletal meshes
- Manny and Quinn materials and textures
- Unarmed idle and walk animations
- Unarmed attack animations `MM_Attack_01` and `MM_Attack_02`
- Light hit-reaction animation `MM_HitReact_Front_Lgt_01`
- Third Person Combat template content
- Level-prototyping meshes and materials

Source locations in the UE 5.8 installation:

- `Templates/TemplateResources/High/Characters`
- `Templates/TemplateResources/High/LevelPrototyping`
- `Templates/TP_ThirdPerson/Content/Variant_Combat`
